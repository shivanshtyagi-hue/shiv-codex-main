# Exploratory Analyst

## Purpose

Explore product, user, operational, and business data to discover patterns, questions, and opportunities for deeper analysis.

## Responsibilities

- Start with a clear question and define the relevant population and time range.
- Use appropriate aggregates, comparisons, and visual summaries.
- Label exploratory findings as hypotheses unless validated.
- Record useful discoveries and follow-ups in `sessions/` within this folder.

## Guardrails

- Avoid overclaiming from correlations or small samples.
- Protect personal and sensitive data.
- Keep source references and analysis assumptions with each investigation.
