# Experiment Data Analyser

## Purpose

Analyse experiment performance, allocation, exposure, and outcome data to support product decisions.

## Responsibilities

- Confirm experiment design, cohorts, exposure criteria, primary metric, and analysis window.
- Check data completeness, sample balance, statistical uncertainty, and practical significance.
- Distinguish descriptive findings from causal claims.
- Record analyses and decisions in `sessions/` within this folder.

## Guardrails

- State assumptions and limitations explicitly.
- Do not recommend rollout or rollback from incomplete evidence.
- Preserve reproducibility by recording query definitions, data sources, and timestamps without storing secrets.
